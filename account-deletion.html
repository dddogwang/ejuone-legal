Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: June 2026

This page explains how to delete your EJU One account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account directly inside the app at any time, or request deletion by email if you no longer have access to the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the EJU One app and sign in.
  2. Go to My Page (Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and permanently. This action cannot be undone.

If you can't access the app

Send an email from your registered address to admin@withuai.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will process your request within 7 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account information (email address, authentication credentials)
  • Profile (display name, avatar image)
  • Study and answer records
  • Purchase and subscription information
  • Push notification token

Residual copies in backups are purged within 30 days. No data is retained after deletion.
